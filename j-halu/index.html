Same sentence, different evidence

A probe can tell you a response contradicts its source. That is a question about differences between items. Whether the representation tracks the evidence is a question about differences within one item — and the two come apart.

To separate them we hold the response fixed, byte for byte, and edit the source underneath it. Nothing is regenerated, so the scored tokens are identical in all three conditions and nothing that moves can be blamed on wording or length.

The window we test

Gurnee et al. (2026) found that language models keep a small set of verbalizable representations — a J-space — that behaves like a global workspace: reportable, holdable, used in reasoning, while most processing runs outside it.

Their instrument, the Jacobian lens, gives every vocabulary token a direction — its row of WᵤJ — in the model's state space; the familiar logit lens is the special case J = I. Our two readable dictionaries are these two lenses, the Jacobian one built on the reference lens checkpoint released for open models alongside that work, SHA-256-pinned. Our sparse Jacobian-basis codes are J-space coordinates at the capacity that work reports (about concepts at a time); the unembedding codes are the analogous sparse codes in the logit-lens member of the family. They read fake in the workspace of a model whose output stayed calm — the reading that makes a hallucination monitor the natural next system to build on this window. This study measures what such a monitor would actually retain.

What compression keeps

Compress the hidden state into k readable coordinates and measure two things separately: how much of the ability to flag the contradicted response survives, and how much of the sensitivity to the deciding evidence survives.

Readable basis

Prediction saturates quickly. Grounding peaks early, low, and then declines as more coordinates are added. Adding coordinates buys prediction and does not buy grounding.

Three domains, no adjudicator

The benchmark records which field was flipped and to what, so “the deciding evidence” is a fact of construction rather than an annotation. No judge is involved.

The control: does readability matter?

Rotate the dictionary. D′ = DQ keeps atom count, norms and the entire Gram matrix, and destroys only the correspondence between a coordinate and a vocabulary token. Same geometry, no meaning.

On a second, source-disjoint cohort, all three frozen rotation seeds reproduce the gap at every k. Vocabulary meaning is not necessary for the effect. But the readable bases bracket the rotations rather than matching them, and the seeds differ from each other — so our preregistered control gate came back , and we do not claim generic sparsity is confirmed.

Do those coordinates name the deciding evidence?

Mostly not, and for one basis they lose to chance-matched controls. We rank each item's coordinates by contribution and ask whether the top ones contain tokens of the flipped value — against a control that samples other source tokens at exactly the same set size.

What this does not show

A page that claims more than its paper is a failed page. Every adverse result below is in the manuscript body, and stays here.

We do not claim that readability causes the loss of grounding; on the confirmation cohort the readable unembedding basis has the highest grounding retention of every basis tested, meaning-free rotations included. We do not claim these coordinates faithfully explain a decision. We do not report a working recovery or mitigation method. The free-generation steering intervention was null and is retained as a boundary condition.
